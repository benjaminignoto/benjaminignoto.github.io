I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the [Institute of Economic Research (IRENE)](https://www.unine.ch/irene/) of the University of Neuchâtel, Switzerland, supervised by [Prof. Bruno Lanz](https://sites.google.com/site/brunolanzwebpage/).

My research is in **Environmental and Climate economics**, with a focus on biodiversity, ecosystem services, and land use. I combine causal inference methods with large-scale spatial and land-use data to study how agricultural practices, conservation investments and protected areas shape biodiversity and resilience to climate change.

You can find my ongoing work on the [Research](/research/) page and my [CV here](/files/Benjamin_Ignoto_CV.pdf).
