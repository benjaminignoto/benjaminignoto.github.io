I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the [Institute of Economic Research (IRENE)](https://www.unine.ch/irene/) of the University of Neuchâtel, Switzerland, supervised by Prof. Bruno Lanz.

My research lies at the intersection of **environmental economics**, **biodiversity** and **agricultural policy**, with broader interests in public economics and political economy. I combine causal inference methods with large-scale spatial and land-use data to study how agricultural practices, conservation investments and protected areas shape biodiversity and resilience to climate change.

In 2025, I was a visiting PhD student at the [MIT Center for Sustainability Science and Strategy](https://cs3.mit.edu/). Before starting my PhD, I was a research intern at the World Trade Organization, where I contributed to the *World Trade Report 2022* on climate change and international trade.

You can find my ongoing work on the [Research](/research/) page and my [CV here](/files/Benjamin_Ignoto_CV.pdf).
